RETURN POLICY

Last updated: January 1, 2026

✅ Hassle-Free Returns — No Restocking Fees

We stand behind every part we sell. If something is wrong, we make it right — quickly and without hassle.

1. Return Eligibility

You may return a part within 30 days of delivery if:

  • The part is in its original, unused condition and original packaging.
  • The part was incorrectly supplied due to our error.
  • The part does not match the VIN or fitment confirmed at the time of order.
  • The part arrived damaged or defective.

Parts that have been installed, modified, or used are not eligible for return unless they are defective.

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Electrical components (sensors, control modules, ECUs) once removed from packaging.
  • Special-order or custom-ordered parts.
  • Parts that have been installed or show signs of use.
  • Parts returned more than 30 days after delivery.
  • Parts without original packaging or missing hardware.

3. How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Contact us at ✉ sales@eliteoemautoparts.com or call +1 (844) 408-3004 within 30 days of delivery.
  • Step 2: Provide your order details, part number (SKU), and reason for return.
  • Step 3: Our team will verify the return eligibility and issue a Return Authorization (RA) number.
  • Step 4: Ship the part back using the address provided. Include the RA number on the package.
  • Step 5: Once we receive and inspect the part, we will process your refund or replacement within 5–7 business days.

4. Wrong Part Delivered

If we supplied the wrong part due to our error, we will:

  • Arrange a free return shipping label for the incorrect part.
  • Ship the correct part at no additional charge.
  • Prioritize your replacement order for same-day dispatch.

5. Damaged or Defective Parts

If your part arrives damaged or is found to be defective:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage.
  • We will arrange a free replacement or full refund — your choice.
  • Do not install a damaged part as this may void the return eligibility.

6. Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected:

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been processed.

7. Return Shipping Costs

  • Our error / defective part: We cover all return shipping costs.
  • Customer change of mind: Customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
  • We recommend using a trackable shipping method for all returns.

8. Contact for Returns

  • 📧 Email: ✉ sales@eliteoemautoparts.com
  • 📞 Phone: +1 (844) 408-3004 (Mon–Fri 8AM–8PM EST)

SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: January 1, 2026

🚚 Fast Shipping to All 50 States

Orders placed before 2PM EST ship the same day. Most customers receive their parts within 1–3 business days.

1. Shipping Coverage

Elite OEM Auto Parts ships to all 50 United States, including Alaska and Hawaii. We currently do not ship internationally.

2. Processing Time

  • In-Stock Parts: Orders confirmed and paid before 2PM EST are processed and dispatched the same business day.
  • Orders after 2PM EST: Dispatched the next business day.
  • Special-Order Parts: Processing time will be communicated at the time of order confirmation — typically 2–5 business days.
  • Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

3. Shipping Timeframes

  • Standard Shipping: 1–3 business days (most of the contiguous USA).
  • Alaska & Hawaii: 3–7 business days.
  • Remote Areas: May require additional transit time.

Delivery timeframes are estimates and may vary due to carrier delays, weather conditions, or other factors outside our control.

4. Shipping Carriers

We ship via the following trusted carriers depending on part size, weight, and destination:

  • FedEx Ground / Express
  • UPS Ground / Express
  • USPS Priority Mail

The carrier is selected at our discretion based on the fastest and most reliable option for your location.

5. Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you will receive:

  • A tracking number via email or SMS (based on your preferred contact method).
  • A direct link to track your shipment in real time.

If you do not receive tracking information within 1 business day of your order confirmation, please contact us at ✉ sales@eliteoemautoparts.com.

6. Packaging

All parts are carefully packaged to prevent damage during transit:

  • Heavy and fragile parts are double-boxed with foam padding.
  • All shipments are insured against loss or damage during transit.
  • Parts are securely sealed to maintain OEM quality during delivery.

7. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated based on part weight, dimensions, and destination. The exact shipping cost will be confirmed at the time of your quote. We offer free shipping on qualifying orders — ask our specialist for details when requesting a quote.

8. Lost or Delayed Shipments

If your shipment appears lost or significantly delayed:

  • Contact us at +1 (844) 408-3004 or ✉ sales@eliteoemautoparts.com.
  • We will open a carrier investigation on your behalf.
  • If confirmed lost, we will ship a replacement at no additional cost.

9. Incorrect Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate at the time of order. If an error is discovered after dispatch, contact us immediately at +1 (844) 408-3004. Address correction fees charged by the carrier may apply.

10. Contact for Shipping Inquiries

  • 📧 Email: ✉ sales@eliteoemautoparts.com
